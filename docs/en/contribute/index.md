# Contribute
 If you like my projects, please consider doing one of the following:
* Purchase **"PDFWallet Pro"**, an in-app purchase available in the PDFWallet apps for [iOS, macOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/6767864357) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.michaeltroger.gruenerpass).
* Purchase **“Cyrillic Pro“**, an in-app purchase available in the Cyrillic Transliterator apps for [iOS, macOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/cyrillic-to-latin/id6758277969) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=at.mikenet.serbianlatintocyrillic).
* Rate my apps with **5 stars** on the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store.
* Recommend my apps to family and friends.
